Mekha

Where are my photos?

In your Google Drive, in the "Mekha" folder, split into Photos / Videos / Screenshots / Documents by year. Albums are sub-folders.

Why can't I see files I dragged into the Mekha folder on the web?

Mekha only has access to files it created (drive.file) for privacy. Upload through Mekha instead.

An upload is stuck

The Uploads tab tells you why. After a dropped connection it resumes by itself; with "Wi‑Fi only" on it waits for Wi‑Fi.

Can I restore deleted files?

Yes, for 30 days from Google Drive's trash.

I forgot my hidden-album passcode

It cannot be recovered: files are encrypted on your device and nobody else holds the key.

Disconnect Google

Settings › Sign out, or myaccount.google.com/permissions
