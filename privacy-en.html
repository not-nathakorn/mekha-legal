Privacy Policy

Mekha · Last updated 23 November 2026

Overview

Mekha manages photos, videos and files stored in your own Google Drive. Mekha has no servers of its own: files go straight from your device to Google. The developer never sees, stores or sells your photos, files or account data. No ads, no trackers.

Data controller

Mekha developer (Codex Studio), codex-studio@outlook.com

What the app accesses

  • Google account email: to show which account is connected; stored in the device Keychain
  • Sign-in tokens (OAuth): to call the Google Drive API on your behalf; stored in the device Keychain
  • Files Mekha created in your Drive (drive.file scope): to show, organise into albums, download, share and star them. Mekha cannot see any other file in your Drive.
  • Photos and files you pick or take with the in-app camera: uploaded to your Drive; the temporary copy is deleted after an MD5 check passes
  • File index, thumbnails and screen-sized copies: kept on the device for speed and offline viewing; clear them in Settings
  • Data-usage counters: counted on the device only, never sent anywhere

Device permissions

  • Camera: only while you use the in-app camera
  • Photos: picked through the system picker (the app only sees what you choose); "add only" access when you save a photo from Drive

Hidden album

Hidden-album files are encrypted (AES-GCM) on the device before upload, with a key derived from your passcode and a secret in your iCloud Keychain. Neither Google nor the developer can read them. Forgotten passcodes cannot be recovered.

Use of Google user data

Mekha's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google Drive data is used only to show and manage your files inside the app. It is never transferred to others, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

Sharing

Your data goes only to Google (the Drive provider), under Google's Privacy Policy. Public share links are created only when you tap and confirm.

Deleting data

  • Sign out (Settings › Sign out): removes tokens, index, caches and upload queue from the device and revokes access at Google
  • Delete the app: removes everything on the device
  • Files in Google Drive are yours; delete them in Drive and revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions

Security

All connections use HTTPS/TLS directly to Google. Your Google password never passes through the app (you sign in on Google's own page).

Your rights

You may request access, correction, deletion or withdraw consent at codex-studio@outlook.com. We reply within 30 days.

Children

Mekha is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

Material changes will be announced in the app and the date above updated.
