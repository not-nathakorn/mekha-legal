Mekha

Your photos, videos and files, kept in your own Google Drive

Mekha is an iPhone, iPad and Mac app that moves your photos and files to your own Google Drive and shows them as a fast, beautiful library. Every byte lives in your Drive; nothing passes through our servers.

Original qualityNo compression; every file MD5-checked against Drive
Tidy foldersPhotos / Videos / Screenshots by year in Drive
Never lostBackground uploads that resume after a dropped connection
Hidden albumEncrypted on device before upload, with a decoy passcode
Built-in cameraShots go straight to Drive
Works offlineScreen-sized copies kept on the device

Google permissions

Mekha requests the drive.file scope, which can only see and manage files Mekha created itself. The rest of your Drive stays invisible to it. It also reads your email address to show which account is connected.
